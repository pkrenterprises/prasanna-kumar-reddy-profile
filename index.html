About M Prasanna Kumar Reddy

Professional background, entrepreneurial journey and leadership association across the PKR Groups ecosystem.

Entrepreneur & Founder

M Prasanna Kumar Reddy began his entrepreneurial journey in 2019, exploring different business ideas and developing practical business experience.

In 2024, he pursued a travel agency venture. From August 2025, he began selling products through Meesho.

PKR Enterprises was established in November 2025, followed by the technology organization that is now represented as VAYUPRABA Technologies.

His current professional interests include entrepreneurship, technology, e-commerce, engineering and enterprise-oriented development.
